# About
I am Will Rosenberg, a computer science PhD student at Arizona State University (ASU) in the SEFCOM lab, advised by Professor Yan Shoshitaishvili. I also work closely with Professors Ruoyu (Fish) Wang, Adam Doupé, and Tiffany Bao.

I enjoy understanding topics from first principles, which has led me to pursue math, physics, and computer science throughout my academic career. This curiosity drew me to the field of computer security, where I dissect technologies to reveal flaws in their design and implementation. My research focuses on software security with an emphasis on automated program analysis and kernel security.

I am a big fan of Capture the Flag (CTF) hacking competitions and compete under the pseudonym "mowteam". I focus on pwn challenges along with the occasional crypto and rev challenge. I currently play with Shellphish and previously captained [b34r5hell](https://wustl-bearshell.github.io) from 2023-2025.

I also contribute to the Great Internet Mersenne Prime Search.
